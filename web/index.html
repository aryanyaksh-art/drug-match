      Method

      How the ranking works

      No machine learning, nothing trained. A fixed, published formula applied to public biology data.

      Two kinds of path

      A drug reaches a disease either directly through a gene it targets, or through one extra step where its target physically interacts with a disease gene.

      Drugtargets Genelinked to Disease
      Drugtargets Gene Ainteracts Gene Blinked to Disease

      Why the extra step matters

      The gene behind a rare disease usually has no drug against it, which is often why the disease has no treatment. Alkaptonuria is caused by a broken HGD gene with zero approved drugs. But HGD interacts with HPD, and HPD's drug is nitisinone, the real approved treatment.

      Adding the hop moved nitisinone from rank 27 to rank 2.

      Busy genes count for less

      A gene hit by hundreds of drugs, or a drug that hits hundreds of genes, tells you very little. So every path is damped by how connected its nodes are.

      score = Σ association(gene, disease)deg(drug)0.4 · deg(gene)0.4

      0.4 is the exponent published by Project Rephetio. We never tuned it.

      No peeking at the answer

      Some gene–disease evidence is derived from drugs that already treat the disease. Using it would be reading the answer off the back of the book, so we exclude the clinical and known_drug evidence types. Every link comes from genetics, animal models, expression data and literature.
